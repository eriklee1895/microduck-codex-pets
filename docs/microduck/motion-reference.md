# MicroDuck motion reference

The pet motion is intentionally based on the small robot's physical construction rather than a generic mascot animation.

- Walking uses short alternating waddling steps, compact hip and knee compression, and controlled weight transfer.
- The head shell and camera lens stay comparatively stable while the exposed neck and lower joints articulate.
- Jumping is a small mechanical hop with joint compression and no cartoon impact effects.
- Waiting and review use small head/neck adjustments and an expectant or focused posture.
- The look-direction loop uses the rigid head shell and articulated neck as the gaze mechanism; the lower body and feet remain anchored.
- The bill remains a thin, flat, nearly closed mechanical strip in every state and direction.
